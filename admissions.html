Admissions Information

Everything you need to know about applying for a place at Weltevreden Park Primary School for the 2026 academic year.

Three Pathways

Choose Your Application Route

The admissions process differs depending on which grade you're applying for. Find your route below.

1
Grade 1

For the 2026 academic year. All Grade 1 applications are processed through the Gauteng Department of Education online portal.

Online via GDE Portal
1
Visit www.gdeadmissions.gov.za and complete your online application. Applications open 24 July 2025 and close 29 August 2025.
2
Once you receive a placement offer from the GDE, collect the WPPS application form from our school office or download it from the website.
3
Return the completed form with all certified supporting documents to the school office in person.
Apply on GDE Portal →
2
Grades 2 — 7

Manual application process. Forms are available directly from the school office and must be submitted in person.

Manual · In-Person Only
1
Collect the application form from the school office during office hours.
2
Complete the form and make certified copies of all required documentation.
3
Return the completed application in person. Completed forms may not be emailed — physical submission is required.
4
Placement is determined via waiting lists. The school will contact you when a place becomes available.
Contact the School
R
Grade R (Pre-Primary)

Our pre-primary is located separately from the main school. Contact us directly to begin your Grade R application.

Contact Directly
Four Grade R classes, 27 learners per class (108 total)
Learners must be 5 turning 6, or 4 turning 5 before 30 June in their Grade R year
School hours: 07h30 — 13h00 (no aftercare available)
Applications for 2026 open 24 July 2025
Address: 15 Krinkhout Avenue, Weltevreden Park 1709
Attending the pre-school does not guarantee a Grade 1 placement at the primary school.
Contact Pre-Primary
Before You Apply

Supporting Documents

All documents must be certified copies. Have these ready before submitting your application.

Certified copy of the learner's birth certificate
Certified copies of parent/guardian identity documents
Proof of residential address (utility bill or bank statement)
Latest school report (for Grades 2—7 applications)
Immunisation record (Road-to-Health booklet)
Enrolment Letter 2026
PDF · Available from school office
Application Forms 2026
PDF · Available from school office
Important Notices

What to Keep in Mind

Grade 1 applications are online only via the GDE portal. The school cannot accept direct Grade 1 applications.
Grades 2—7 applications cannot be emailed — physical submission to the school office is required.
Grade R placement at the pre-primary school does not guarantee a Grade 1 spot at the primary school.
Outstanding school fees affect credit bureau records. Contact the school about fee exemption options if required.
