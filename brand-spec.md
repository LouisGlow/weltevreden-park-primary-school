## Typography

- **Display:** 'Lora', Georgia, serif — warm, slightly bookish, trustworthy
- **Body:** 'Inter', -apple-system, system-ui, sans-serif — clean, readable
- **Mono:** ui-monospace, 'JetBrains Mono', monospace — metadata only

## Layout Posture

1. Warm off-white page background (not pure white — feels institutional if too stark)
2. Royal blue as the dominant accent: primary CTA buttons, active nav, section headings eyebrow
3. Warm gold as decorative highlight: blockquote borders, secondary badges, hero frosted panel
4. Orange-red used sparingly: house color, danger/alert states only
5. Generous whitespace — school content is information-dense, let it breathe
6. Rounded corners (8–14px) — friendly but not childlike
7. No gradients on content sections — only on hero and dark-bg sections
8. Serif headlines, sans-serif body — confidence + readability

## One-line summary

Warm cream background · royal blue (oklch 46%/0.19/258) as primary · warm gold (68%/0.14/82) as decorative secondary · deep navy text · Lora display + Inter body.
