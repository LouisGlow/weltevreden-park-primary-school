Assistance Available

Fee Exemptions

Parents who cannot afford the full fee are encouraged to apply for a fee exemption. The process is confidential.

Exemption applications are available from the school office
Requires bank verification and recent stamped bank statements
Applications are assessed individually and confidentially
Partial exemptions may be granted based on means testing
Contact the School Office
Important Notes

Payment Policy

Monthly instalments are due at the beginning of each month, February through November.
Early-payment discounts only apply to lump-sum payments and require payment by the stated deadline.
Outstanding fees affect credit bureau records. Please contact the school immediately if you are experiencing payment difficulties.
Banking details for fee payments are available from the school office only. Do not pay to any other account without written confirmation.
