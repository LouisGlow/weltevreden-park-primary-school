## About / History

Weltevreden Park Primary School commenced operations on 7 January 1987, welcoming 209 students under Principal Willie Botha's leadership. The institution initially served grades through Grade 6 and operated with minimal resources — "filing was done out of apple boxes."

The school's competitive houses were originally named Martians, Quasars, and Comets, later renamed Falcons, Eagles, and Hawks in 1988, with Kestrels added in 1993.

Subsequent principals drove expansion. Piet Roodt (1988–1990) implemented infrastructure improvements, including acquiring a lawnmower to replace the sheep that had previously grazed the fields. Clive Fitch (1988–1992) and David Anderson (1992–1996) oversaw significant growth requiring prefabricated classrooms. Under Anderson, the swimming pool opened in 1993, followed by tuckshop and office extensions.

Lex Morton assumed leadership in July 1996, guiding enrolment to nearly 1,400 students. His tenure saw three new classrooms (2001), pool facilities improvements, floodlit sports fields, and southern fields opening (2003). The Pavilion, constructed in 2007, was named "The Danie Volkwyn Pavilion" honouring a former Deputy Principal. The staffroom expanded in 2015.

Morton's retirement at year-end 2015 prompted renaming the school hall "The Lex Morton Hall" in tribute. James Van Dyk became the sixth principal during second term 2016.

## Mission Statement & Goals

**Mission Statement:** "With a dedicated and professional staff and a committed parent body, W.P.P.S. strives to provide quality education and develop social skills through maximum involvement, self-discipline and effort."

**Guiding Motto:** "Growth in Harmony"

**Seven Goals:**
1. Delivering the highest quality education available
2. Emphasising social development for well-rounded student growth
3. Maintaining professional educators who respect student individuality and create tailored learning experiences
4. Prioritising creativity and lateral thinking in curricula
5. Encouraging pupils to maintain "high standards through self-discipline and effort"
6. Fostering robust parental participation to build community awareness
7. Developing comprehensive extramural activities contributing to balanced education

**Vision:** The school invites all stakeholders to participate in "our vision to build a school community of excellence and tradition," reflecting commitment to creating an integrated educational environment where professional educators, engaged families, and motivated students work collaboratively toward student success.
