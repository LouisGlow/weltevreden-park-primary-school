A Message from the Principal

A Place Where Every Child Grows

"With a dedicated and professional staff and a committed parent body, WPPS strives to provide quality education and develop social skills through maximum involvement, self-discipline and effort."

Since opening its doors on 7 January 1987 with 209 learners, Weltevreden Park Primary School has grown into a cornerstone of this community. What began with filing done out of apple boxes and sheep grazing the playing fields has become a school of excellence and tradition.

Mr Van Dyk became our sixth principal during the second term of 2016, continuing a legacy that spans nearly four decades. He leads both the primary and pre-primary divisions with the same commitment our founders instilled from day one.

Our Seven Goals

  1. Delivering the highest quality education available
  2. Emphasising social development for well-rounded growth
  3. Maintaining professional educators who respect individual learners
  4. Prioritising creativity and lateral thinking in curricula
  5. Encouraging high standards through self-discipline and effort
  6. Fostering robust parental participation and community awareness
  7. Developing comprehensive extramural activities for balanced education
